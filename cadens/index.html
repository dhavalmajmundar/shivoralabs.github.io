What makes Cadens different

Built around recovery,
not rigid schedules

Most apps track what you did. Cadens tracks how you're doing — and adjusts what comes next.

🧠

Adaptive Training Plans

Daily recovery check-ins feed an AI coach that reshuffles your week when you're sore, tired, or under the weather.

📍

GPS Activity Tracking

Accurate GPS for runs, walks, cycles, and hikes. Live pace, distance, and elevation — with auto-pause when you stop.

⏱

Interval Workouts

Build custom HIIT, Tabata, and rest-interval sequences with audio coaching cues and live countdown overlays.

📊

Weekly Progress Reports

Shareable weekly summaries with distance, calories, active minutes, and trend comparisons to the prior week.

⌚

Health Connect Sync

Reads steps, floors, heart rate, and sleep from Health Connect — including Samsung Health and Fitbit data.

🎽

Gear Tracker

Log shoes, vests, and equipment. Set mileage alerts so you know exactly when to replace your running shoes.

Activity modes built for real life

Not every workout happens on a track. We built dedicated modes for the things people actually do.

🐾

Dog Walk Mode

Tracks sniff stops, leash pull events, and walking rhythm — giving you a picture of how your dog is doing alongside your own activity data.

Beta exclusive
🚜

Lawn Care Tracking

GPS-mapped coverage area, equipment profile (brand, model, fuel type), and calorie burn — so your yard work actually counts toward your weekly totals.

Beta exclusive
🏃

Treadmill Mode

Step-counted distance when GPS isn't available. Set your speed manually or let the pedometer handle it. Includes interval overlays and audio coaching.
