Cadens Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Cadens is operated by Shivora Labs LLC ("Shivora Labs", "we", "us", or "our").

Cadens is an adaptive fitness, walking, workout, and consistency tracking app that helps users manage routines, review workout metrics, and follow training plans.

For questions regarding this policy, contact us at support@shivoralabs.com.

2. Information you provide

  • Account details: email address and display name. A username is derived automatically from your display name and can be edited.
  • Optional profile details: first and last name, phone number, date of birth, postal address, and emergency contact. These are optional and can be left blank or cleared.
  • Optional content: profile photo, workout photos, workout notes, goals, gear details, and feedback you submit.
← Previous: 1. Who we are Next: 3. Information collected →

3. Information collected when you use the app

  • Activity data: workout type, start and end time, duration, distance, pace, speed, elevation, step count, lap splits, and related workout metrics.
  • Location data: GPS coordinates recorded during an activity you start, including while the app is in the background if you grant background location. Location is not collected when no activity is running.
  • Health data: if you enable Health Connect, Cadens reads heart rate, distance, steps, calories, floors climbed, and activity intensity data for the exact time window of a workout you recorded. Cadens does not read your wider health history.
  • Approximate location for weather: a coarse location is used to fetch local conditions and forecast guidance.
  • Diagnostics: app version, device model, operating-system version, permission states, and error events, used to fix crashes and reliability problems.
← Previous: 2. Information you provide Next: 4. How used →

4. How your information is used

  • To display workout history, weekly summaries, rhythm waves, maps, and activity trends.
  • To generate adaptive workout suggestions, interval targets, and recovery hints.
  • To manage your account, verify your email, authenticate logins, and handle password resets.
  • To sync data securely across your devices.
  • To monitor app performance, debug crashes, and prevent abuse.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your health or location data for third-party advertising.

5. AI coaching and smart features

Cadens includes adaptive coaching suggestions based on workout completion history, stated goals, recent activity intensity, and self-reported recovery or pain signals.

Coaching guidance is generated to help you plan workouts safely. It is not medical or healthcare advice. You can disable adaptive coaching features at any time in app settings.

← Previous: 4. How used Next: 6. Storage & security →

6. Data storage, retention, and security

Your data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (TLS) and stored in secure cloud infrastructure with access controls and database-level security rules.

You can export or delete your account and all associated workout data directly within the app under Profile → Settings & Tools, or by emailing support@shivoralabs.com.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and Export: download a copy of your workout and profile data in a standard format.
  • Correction: edit display name, profile details, and workout logs directly in the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual workouts or your entire account and all stored history at any time.
  • Permissions: revoke location, notification, or Health Connect permissions at any time through device settings.
